◆ THE CORE PREMISE

DSA's Got Latent is a teaching and creative visualization challenge that takes DSA beyond conventional coding and problem-solving.

PHASE 01 1-WEEK VISUAL SPRINT

Participants will choose a DSA topic and get approximately one week to understand it, explore its applications, and create a creative visual representation using posters, models, diagrams, demonstrations, analogies, or any other medium.

POSTERS MODELS DIAGRAMS DEMOS ANALOGIES
PHASE 02 LIVE STAGE DEFENSE

During the event, participants must teach their topic completely from scratch, making it understandable even to someone with no prior knowledge. They will be evaluated on their conceptual clarity, teaching ability, creativity, visual representation, and communication.

CLARITY TEACHING CREATIVITY VISUALS COMMUNICATION
◆ THE LATENT GAMBIT // SELF-PREDICTION RULE WINNER TAKE ALL

To make things even more interesting, participants will also predict their own final score before the judges reveal it. If the predicted score matches the final judges' score, the team wins the challenge!
